# BRIEF

## Product

Lobster Agent Trader — a self-hosted web app that turns the AI token allowance you never
spend into a disciplined trading loop on Lighter.

## Intent

Show-it-as-is product preview. The captured console screens are the visual source of truth;
the video features them rather than reinterpreting them.

## Format

- **Aspect ratio:** 1:1 (square)
- **Resolution:** 1080x1080
- **Duration:** 30 seconds
- **Language:** English

## Audience

Developers and researchers who pay for an AI token subscription and let part of it expire
unused each month. Secondary: builders evaluating automated trading systems.

## Core message

You already pay for tokens that quietly expire. This spends them, under hard limits, with a
record of every decision — and it runs on your own machine.

## Beat sheet

1. **Hook** — tokens expire unnoticed
2. **What it is** — one loop, stated plainly
3. **Evidence** — the real console: dashboard, runs, agent
4. **Proof of discipline** — guardrails and the three live locks
5. **Close** — self-hosted, bring your own keys, repository link

## Assets

Real captured screenshots of the running console, light and dark, plus the exported brand
mark. No fabricated UI, no mock dashboards.

## Voice

Calm, precise, engineering-voice. States what the software does without claiming returns.
No hype language, no "revolutionary", no performance promises.

## Audio

- **Narration:** English voice, local engine (HeyGen not signed in)
- **Music:** understated electronic, confident rather than energetic
- **Captions:** on

## Design direction

Follow the product's own design language: light canvas `#f2f0f3`, near-black ink `#12101c`,
the mint tint `#7ff0e2` as the single accent, and the dark canvas `#0e0b1a` for the dark
beats. Type is the brand's own sans. The video should feel like the product's own marketing
site, not a separate motion brand.

## Claims to avoid

- No return or profit claims
- No suggestion that paper results predict live results
- No "guaranteed", "risk-free", "proven profitable"
