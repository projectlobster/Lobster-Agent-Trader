[h2] Start from your monthly allowance
[p] You already pay for a monthly AI token budget. Whatever you do not spend on ordinary work normally just expires. Lobster Agent Trader turns that remainder into an explicit number the agent is allowed to burn, and stops the moment it runs out.
[li] budget − reserved for real work = tradable allowance
[li] Every cycle's token spend is recorded, per model and per day
[li] Unused allowance can carry into the next period
